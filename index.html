隐私政策

Astrolabe · 星枢 是一款离线优先的 AR 星空仪。本政策说明本应用收集、使用和共享哪些数据。

最后更新：2026 年 7 月 18 日 · 生效日期：2026 年 7 月 18 日

核心承诺：本应用不上传您的个人数据，不接入任何第三方分析、广告或追踪 SDK， 不要求账号、不收集设备标识符。相机画面、位置坐标、天体计算结果全部在您的设备上本地处理。

1. 我们是谁

Astrolabe · 星枢（以下简称"本应用"）由个人开发者 张飞洋 开发和发布。 联系邮箱：zfy1173205582@gmail.com。

2. 我们收集的数据

2.1 您主动授权使用的数据（仅在设备本地处理，不上传）

数据类型用途是否上传
相机画面 在 AR 取景画面上叠加星空层 否，仅在设备内存中实时处理，不录制、不存储、不上传
位置坐标（经纬度） 计算天体的方位角、高度角，以及本地天文事件（日出/日落、月相、卫星过境等） 否，仅本地计算，不写入文件、不上传
设备方向传感器数据 确定相机光轴指向天空的真实方位（结合磁偏角修正） 否，仅本地处理

2.2 应用主动获取的公开数据（不包含您的任何信息）

数据来源内容频率关联账号
CelesTrak（公共 TLE 数据） 卫星轨道两行根数（TLE），用于在 AR 视图中渲染卫星轨迹 每日 1–2 次，结果本地缓存 无
Open-Meteo（公共天气 API） 当前位置的天气预报（云量、湿度），用于"观测条件"建议 用户进入"观测"页面时，结果本地缓存 无

这两个 API 都是无需账号的公共服务，仅传出您的公网 IP 和标准 User-Agent 头， 不附加任何设备或用户标识。我们不会向这些服务发送您设备上存储的其他数据。

3. 我们不收集的数据

  • 不收集姓名、邮箱、电话、地址等个人身份信息（您主动通过"邮件反馈"提供的除外）
  • 不收集设备唯一标识符（IMEI、Android ID、AAID、IDFA 等）
  • 不接入任何分析 SDK（无 Google Analytics、Firebase、友盟、Sentry 等）
  • 不接入任何广告 SDK（无 AdMob、穿山甲、广点通等）
  • 不接入任何社交分享或第三方登录
  • 不记录您的相机画面、屏幕截图、键盘输入
  • 不读取您的通讯录、相册、麦克风

TLE 和天气预报的缓存文件保存在应用私有缓存目录，遵循 Android 系统缓存清理策略，不会主动上传。

Privacy Policy

Astrolabe · 星枢 is an offline-first AR planetarium. This policy describes what data the app collects, uses, and shares.

Last updated: July 18, 2026 · Effective: July 18, 2026

Core commitment: this app does not upload your personal data, integrates no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDK, requires no account, and collects no device identifiers. Camera frames, location coordinates, and sky calculations are all processed locally on your device.

1. Who we are

Astrolabe · 星枢 (the "App") is developed and published by individual developer Zhang Feiyang. Contact: zfy1173205582@gmail.com.

2. Data we collect

2.1 Data you grant access to (processed on-device only, never uploaded)

DataPurposeUploaded?
Camera frames Overlay sky objects on the AR viewfinder No — processed in memory in real time, never recorded, stored, or uploaded
Location (lat/long) Compute azimuth/altitude of celestial objects, plus local events (sunrise, moon phase, satellite passes) No — local calculation only, not written to disk, not uploaded
Device orientation sensors Determine the camera's true pointing direction (with magnetic declination correction) No — local processing only

2.2 Public data the app fetches (no personal information involved)

SourceContentFrequencyAccount?
CelesTrak (public TLE) Two-Line Element sets for satellite orbits, used to render satellite tracks in AR 1–2× per day, cached locally None
Open-Meteo (public weather API) Local weather forecast (cloud cover, humidity) for "observation conditions" When user opens the Observation screen; cached locally None

Both APIs are public, account-free services. Only your public IP and a standard User-Agent header are sent — no device or user identifier is attached. We do not send any other on-device data to these services.

3. Data we do NOT collect

  • No personally identifiable information (name, email, phone, address) unless you contact us by email
  • No device identifiers (IMEI, Android ID, AAID, IDFA, etc.)
  • No analytics SDK (no Google Analytics, Firebase, Umeng, Sentry, etc.)
  • No advertising SDK (no AdMob, Pangle, Tencent Ads, etc.)
  • No social sharing or third-party login
  • No recording of your camera, screenshots, or keystrokes
  • No access to your contacts, photo library, or microphone

4. Permissions

The app declares the following permissions in its manifest:

PermissionWhyRequired?
CAMERA AR viewfinder preview Yes (denying disables AR mode, but Calendar / Search still work)
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION Get lat/long to compute visible sky Yes (denying falls back to a default location, and you can pick a city manually)
INTERNET Fetch TLE and weather data Optional (cached data still works offline)

All permissions are requested at runtime. You may revoke any of them at any time in system settings.

5. Data storage and retention

The App has no server-side database. All user data (manually entered coordinates, favorites, last selected city) is stored only in the app's private directory and is removed when you uninstall the App.

Cached TLE and weather files live in the private cache directory and follow the OS's normal cache eviction policy. They are never uploaded.

6. Third-party services

The App uses the following third-party public services; each has its own privacy policy:

  • CelesTrak: celestrak.org (no login, no cookies)
  • Open-Meteo: open-meteo.com/en/privacy

We do not share data with these services — they only see your public IP and standard HTTP request headers.

7. Children's privacy

The App is for general audiences and is not targeted at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided personal information without your consent, please contact zfy1173205582@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.

8. Your rights

Because we store no user data on any server, "delete my data" can be done in any of these ways:

  • System Settings → Apps → Astrolabe → Clear data
  • Uninstall the App
  • Within the App, clear manual coordinates and city selection under "Location/Display"

For help, contact zfy1173205582@gmail.com. We will respond within 7 business days.

9. Changes to this policy

If we make a material change to this policy, we will show an in-app notice and ask you to re-confirm. Minor changes (e.g. updating contact details) will only be reflected on this page and in the "Last updated" date.

10. Governing law

This policy is governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China, and is also informed by the principles of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).
