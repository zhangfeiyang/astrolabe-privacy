Astrolabe for iPhone — Privacy Policy

App: Astrolabe · 星枢 (iOS, bundle ID com.astro.simulator) · Last updated: October 17, 2026

In short: Astrolabe has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your camera image, location and motion data are processed on your iPhone. The only thing that leaves the device is an approximate location (rounded to about 1 km) sent to a public weather service to fetch the cloud forecast.

1. Who we are

Astrolabe is developed and published by the individual developer Zhang Feiyang. Contact: zfy1173205582@gmail.com.

2. Data processed on your device only

DataPurposeLeaves the device?
Camera image (only if you allow camera access)Shows the real sky behind the star labels in AR viewNo. Processed live in memory; never recorded, stored or uploaded.
Location (only if you allow location access)Computes which stars, planets and events are visible where you are; light-pollution class from a bundled mapOnly a rounded copy for the weather forecast (see section 3).
Motion sensors and compassWork out where the phone is pointingNo.
Settings (display options, chosen city or coordinates)Remember your preferencesNo. Stored only in the app's private storage on the device.

You can use Astrolabe without camera or location access: the star chart still works, and you can pick a city or enter coordinates manually.

3. Data sent to third parties

To show cloud cover and the observing index, the app requests a forecast from Open-Meteo, a free public weather API. The request contains the latitude and longitude rounded to two decimal places (about 1 km) and the standard network information every internet request carries (such as your IP address). No account, device identifier or any other personal data is attached. Open-Meteo's own terms and privacy policy apply: open-meteo.com/en/terms.

When you tap "Open in maps" for a darker site, the app opens Apple Maps with that point; when you add an event to your calendar, the iOS system calendar editor is used and you decide whether to save it. The app itself never reads your calendar.

4. Data we do not collect

  • No name, email, phone number or other contact details.
  • No advertising identifier (IDFA) or other device identifier; no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or social SDKs.
  • No access to your photos, contacts, microphone or health data.

5. Retention and deletion

There is no server and no user database. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device. You can also revoke camera or location access at any time in iOS Settings → Astrolabe.

6. Children

Astrolabe is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

8. Contact

Questions or requests: zfy1173205582@gmail.com. We reply within 7 business days.

Astrolabe · 星枢（iPhone 版）隐私政策

应用：Astrolabe · 星枢（iOS，Bundle ID com.astro.simulator）· 最后更新：2026 年 10 月 17 日

概要：本应用没有账号、没有广告、没有统计分析、不做任何追踪。相机画面、位置和运动传感器数据都只在你的 iPhone 上处理。唯一离开设备的是一个约 1 公里精度的粗略位置，用于向公共天气服务查询云量预报。

1. 我们是谁

本应用由个人开发者张飞洋开发和发布。联系邮箱：zfy1173205582@gmail.com。

2. 仅在设备本地处理的数据

数据用途是否离开设备
相机画面（仅在你允许相机权限时）在 AR 视图中把星名标注叠加到真实天空上否。仅在内存中实时处理，不录制、不存储、不上传。
位置（仅在你允许定位权限时）计算你所在地可见的恒星、行星与天象；根据内置地图估算光害等级仅将取整后的坐标用于天气预报（见第 3 条）。
运动传感器与指南针判断手机指向天空的方向否。
设置（显示选项、选择的城市或手动坐标）记住你的偏好否。仅保存在本设备的应用私有存储中。

不授予相机或定位权限也可以使用本应用：星图照常工作，你可以选择城市或手动输入经纬度。

3. 发送给第三方的数据

为了显示云量和观星指数，应用会向免费的公共天气接口 Open-Meteo 查询预报。请求中只包含保留两位小数（约 1 公里精度）的经纬度，以及任何网络请求都会带有的标准网络信息（如 IP 地址）；不附带账号、设备标识符或其他个人数据。Open-Meteo 的条款与隐私政策见 open-meteo.com/en/terms。

当你点击暗夜地点的「在地图中打开」时，应用会用 Apple 地图打开该位置；当你把天象加入日历时，使用的是 iOS 系统的日历编辑界面，是否保存由你决定，应用本身从不读取你的日历。

4. 我们不收集的数据

  • 不收集姓名、邮箱、电话等联系方式。
  • 不收集广告标识符（IDFA）或其他设备标识符，不跨应用或网站追踪。
  • 不接入任何统计、崩溃上报、广告或社交 SDK。
  • 不访问照片、通讯录、麦克风或健康数据。

5. 保留与删除

本应用没有服务器，也没有用户数据库。删除应用即删除它在设备上保存的全部内容。你也可以随时在「设置 → Astrolabe」中撤销相机或定位权限。

6. 儿童

本应用适合所有年龄段使用，不会故意收集儿童的个人信息。
