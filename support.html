Frequently asked questions

The labels don't line up with the real sky. Move the phone in a figure-eight to calibrate the compass, keep it away from metal and magnets, and use Settings → Manual alignment for a fine correction.

Can I use it without the camera or location? Yes. Without the camera you get a full star chart; drag to look around and pinch to zoom. Without location, choose a city or enter coordinates in Settings.

Does it work offline? Yes. Star catalogues, planet positions and events are computed on the phone. Only the cloud forecast needs a connection.

How do I change the language? iOS Settings → Astrolabe → Language.

常见问题

标注和真实天空对不上？请用「8 字形」晃动手机校准指南针，远离金属和磁铁；也可以在「设置 → 手动微调」里精细校正。

不开相机或定位能用吗？可以。不开相机时是一张完整的星图，拖动环视、双指缩放；不开定位时可在设置中选择城市或输入经纬度。

能离线使用吗？可以。星表、行星位置与天象都在手机上计算，只有云量预报需要联网。

如何切换语言？iOS「设置 → Astrolabe → 语言」。
